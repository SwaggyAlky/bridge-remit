START HERE

Start with a Test Wallet

Five steps to a verifiable transfer demo.

  1. Install MetaMask and switch to the configured network. Use a test wallet with test ETH for network fees.
  2. Connect your wallet and sign the login message. On first use, select Register as Customer and obtain administrator approval.
  3. Request test dUSD and approve the escrow contract for your planned payment amount.
  4. Enter an approved recipient address, review platform and network fees, and sign to create the transfer. The recipient signs in with their wallet to claim it.
  5. View status in Transactions & Receipts. Explore cancellation, disputes, administrator decisions and agent attestations.

Fees & Trust Assumptions

Transfers use dUSD on one test chain. Cross-border refers to payment corridors, with no cross-chain transfers. Administrators maintain indicative rates; deposits and claims use dUSD. There are no real deposits, bank payouts or automated compliance checks. Do not enter real identity information.

Only the administrator can resolve disputed funds. Senders can cancel pending transfers or obtain a refund after expiry. Claimed transfers cannot be reversed. On-chain data is public, and wallets and hashes may be linked. Payment memos stay in the database; customers can read only their own drafts.

Demo & On-chain Setup

View Demo displays built-in sample data without connecting a wallet or sending transactions. An on-chain demo requires deployed contracts; the network label reflects the configuration. Render serves the website and API, Postgres stores business data, and the blockchain stores fund states.
